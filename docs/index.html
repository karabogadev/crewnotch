Private by design.

CrewNotch is a local app. It watches your agents through hooks and log files on your Mac, and talks to them over a socket only your user can open.

  • No account, no analytics. Nothing about your work is collected or sent anywhere.
  • Observe-only hooks. If CrewNotch isn't running, the hook exits quietly and your agent carries on as usual.
  • Usage is opt-in, per provider. Each one says exactly what it reads. Sign-ins are never refreshed, written or stored.
  • Open source. Every line is on GitHub under Apache 2.0.

Questions

Does it work without a notch?

Yes. On Macs and displays without a notch, CrewNotch draws the same island at the top center of the screen. You can pick which display shows it in Settings → General.

Will it slow down or change my agents?

No. Hooks only report what's happening. They never change an agent's behavior, apart from an approval you give from the notch, and they exit right away if CrewNotch isn't running.

Codex doesn't show up.

Codex asks you to review new hooks the next time it starts. Choose Trust all and continue.

Antigravity or OpenCode doesn't show up.

Both load hooks and plugins at startup. Quit and reopen the app after connecting it.

Kiro doesn't show up.

Kiro only runs global hooks with its v3 engine. Start it with kiro-cli chat --v3.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Settings → Agents and press Disconnect for each agent, which removes CrewNotch's hooks and plugin files. Then quit CrewNotch, move it to the Trash and delete ~/.crewnotch.
